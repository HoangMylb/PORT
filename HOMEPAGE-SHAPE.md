# Homepage shape — Hoàng Mỹ

## Job and audience

- **Surface:** Home, a brand/marketing personal-portfolio surface in **Experience** mode.
- **Audience:** recruiters and engineering leaders evaluating role fit; founders, agencies, and remote clients evaluating delivery confidence.
- **Visitor job:** grasp the frontend specialization in seconds, then inspect credible work and reach a contact route without wading through a CV-like inventory.

## Outcome and proof

- **Primary outcome:** convert curiosity into professional confidence, then into an inspection of selected work or direct contact.
- **Core message:** Hoàng Mỹ is a React/Next.js Frontend Specialist who builds polished, product-aware interfaces and can diagnose across frontend/API/.NET boundaries.
- **Proof available:** MDOP and THÈM GÌ CŨNG CÓ project narratives; 2.5+ years of professional development; explicit engineering practices and real contact/social links. No commercial metrics or client proof may be invented.

## Scope and boundaries

- Full homepage only: Header → Hero → Selected Work → Expertise → Experience / How I Work → Problem Solving → About → CTA → Footer.
- Keep top-level navigation minimal: Work, About, Experience, Contact; resume is visibly pending, not a dead CTA.
- Build target once a direction is chosen: Next.js + TypeScript + GSAP, responsive desktop/tablet/mobile, accessible, performant, with native scrolling retained.
- Anti-goals: CV clone, generic SaaS landing page, tech-logo wall, fake proof, dashboard chrome, heavy card nesting, cyberpunk/gaming visual language.

## Interaction and responsive intent

- Desktop earns depth through editorial scale, project-led storytelling, restrained hover states, and scroll-led reveals.
- Mobile recomposes content into a single clear sequence; critical metadata and contact stay visible without hover or small-text dependence.
- Motion reinforces hierarchy only: reveal, mask, image framing, or controlled parallax; `prefers-reduced-motion` preserves the complete reading experience.
