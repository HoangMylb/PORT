## Product Principles

1. Let substantive project and engineering evidence lead ahead of a technology inventory.
2. Show both sides of the practice: how the interface works and how it feels.
3. Make scanning effortless first, then reward deeper investigation with credible case-study detail.
4. Treat practical problem-solving—understand, diagnose, build, improve—as equal evidence to greenfield work.
5. Let frontend specialization remain unmistakable while backend experience adds confidence rather than competing positioning.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
